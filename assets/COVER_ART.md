# Cover artwork provenance

The cover combines the original Rayls mark, a text-free generated illustration, and deterministic SVG labels. The three data figures are vector layouts populated from the study's saved outputs; they do not use generated evidence or simulated Explorer screenshots.

Art direction for the illustration: two overlapping clipped-corner plates, front matte charcoal with lime perforations, back violet frosted acetate. Three aligned lavender bars and one lime fold-out tab suggest fields sealed inside a document. Use shallow isometric perspective, sharp silhouettes, no signage or logo, no cast shadow, a transparent background and a measured editorial palette. The older DAVID cover was supplied as a style reference. The selected illustration was cropped and resized before being embedded in the SVG.
